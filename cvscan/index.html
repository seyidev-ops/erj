What gets scanned

The ten points, and why each one costs you

Every point below is scored against your uploaded file. The wording is the criterion; the note underneath is what it means in practice.

01

Clear Timezone & Location

Does your contact section state your city, country, and UTC timezone (e.g., Abuja, Nigeria | UTC+1)? Remote employers need to assess working hour overlaps immediately.

02

The "Remote-Ready" Summary

Does your professional summary explicitly highlight your ability to work autonomously, manage cross-border projects, or thrive in distributed teams?

03

Remote Tool Stack

Is there a dedicated "Tools" or "Technology" section that lists standard remote collaboration software you are proficient in (e.g., Slack, Jira, Asana, Zoom, Google Workspace)?

04

ATS-Optimized Formatting

Is your CV free of complex tables, graphics, text boxes, and unusual fonts? Applicant Tracking Systems struggle to read heavily formatted documents. Stick to a clean, single-column minimalist layout.

05

Quantifiable Metrics

Are your achievements measured in numbers? Instead of "managed a team," use "managed a 5-person distributed team to increase revenue by 15% in Q3."

06

Evidence of Asynchronous Communication

Do your bullet points demonstrate your ability to document processes, write clear briefs, and drive projects forward without needing constant live supervision?

07

Strong Action Verbs

Do your experience bullet points start with high-impact, proactive verbs (e.g., Architected, Streamlined, Delivered, Spearheaded) rather than passive phrases (e.g., Responsible for)?

08

Keyword Alignment

Have you mirrored the specific keywords and phrasing used in the target remote job description within your experience and skills sections?

09

Clickable, Professional Links

Are your LinkedIn profile, GitHub repository, and personal portfolio URLs hyperlinked correctly, and do they lead to active, up-to-date pages?

10

Universal File Format

Are you exporting and submitting your CV as a clean PDF (unless specifically asked for a Word document) to ensure your minimalist formatting remains locked across any device or operating system?
