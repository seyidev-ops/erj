Questions

Before you enrol them.

What if they can’t attend the next cohort?

We hold their seat for a later cohort at the same fee. Tell us when they can start.

Does this guarantee them a job?

No. No honest programme can promise a job, a date or a salary. What they get is the preparation, their work marked by a person, and our commitment to keep working with them until they’re hired.

Who is a good fit?

Someone with real work experience, even one or two years, who will turn up for a live class every day for twenty days. If they are not ready for that yet, ask us first and we’ll suggest the right starting point.

Can I pay for something smaller instead?

Yes. You can pay for an Interview Sprint before an interview they have booked, or The Job Hunter. Tell us on WhatsApp who it is for.
