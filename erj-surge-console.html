Diagnosis before prescription

The one sales workflow

A new lead does not receive a product list. Establish the target, read the last 30 days, inspect representation, name the earliest leak, give one useful action, then open the smallest honest paid door.

01 · Target role

One title/field. If they cannot name one, the conversation is not ready for pricing.

02 · 30-day signal

How many applications? How many interviews? This separates silence from mismatch from conversion.

03 · Representation

Read the CV/LinkedIn against the target. Do not assume “CV problem” just because they have a CV.

04 · Earliest leak

Supply → no legitimate roles. Representation → applications, no interviews. Aim → rejections/mismatch. Conversion → interviews, no offer.

05 · First useful action

Give one fix they can act on immediately. This proves the diagnosis before the sale.

06 · Smallest correct door

Only now recommend a paid rung, state its limit, and ask for a clean decision.

02 Representation — the employer cannot read your value from the CV/LinkedIn.
