How ERJ sells now · from 14 November 2026

One free room a week. One clear offer.

Everything public leads to the Saturday Launchpad. The Launchpad is where we audit CVs live and make one offer, once, at the end. The main offer is Remote Job Foundation Training. Nothing else is given away in private.

The rule that changedMore than sixty people got a free private AUDIT and none of them paid. A private AUDIT is now part of Foundation Training and every programme above it (Inner Circle, Placement Engine, Dream Job Offer), done by the participant's facilitator. Anyone else who asks for a CV review gets the Launchpad link. People already halfway through an AUDIT on WhatsApp are finished there; no new ones start.

The weekly rhythm

Sunday to Saturday, every week

Jobs go out at 5:45 AM and 7:45 PM whatever else happens. The Launchpad push uses 12:30 PM (Channel) and 8:00 PM (Status) so the job slots stay clear.

  • Sun
    The honest poll. Channel poll about the state of their CV; text Status at 8 PM. Build this week's warm list from last Saturday's attendees and anyone who sent LAUNCHPAD.
  • Mon
    The kitchen-table test. Voice note on the Channel; Status at 8 PM. Personal messages to five employed contacts, one at a time.
  • Tue
    Spot the call-back. Two opening lines, A or B, as a poll. Answer tomorrow.
  • Wed
    The reveal. What, proof, where. Status for shy people: camera optional, CV shown only with consent.
  • Thu
    Send it ugly. Register, then upload the CV as it is. The one-to-one invite to people in your own network.
  • Fri
    Prepare. 9 AM: export the Zoom registrant list and send the reminder. 6 PM: close uploads and match each CV to a registrant by email. Night: one line per CV (the earliest weak part, the one thing to fix first, and the door that fits).
  • Sat
    The Launchpad. Morning reminder. 3:30 PM open the room. 4:00 PM live. The offer once, at the end. Record every attendee before you sleep.

Where the week leaks
  • Few registrations

    The posts are not reaching the right people, or Thursday's ask was weak. Check the poll counts.

  • Registrations, few CVs

    The upload step is the problem. Send the Friday reminder with the upload link alone.

  • CVs, few attendees

    Timing or reminders. Send the 3:45 PM nudge with the personal Zoom link instruction.

  • Attendees, no offers

    The session ran out of time or the offer was skipped. The offer is not optional.

  • Offers, no payments

    Read their words before touching the product or price.

Before, during and after

The main offer

Remote Job Foundation Training · ₦250,000

Offer it only after the four questions. It is for someone with real work behind them, a target, a date and the means to pay.

What they get

Stages 1–4 live over twenty training days · sixteen submittable assets · work marked by a person · the certificate · 90 days of one Mentorine career track · a private AUDIT of their search by their facilitator · Inner Circle eligibility after graduation.

What it is not

Not a job guarantee, not a date, not a salary. Not a whole new profession (that is Capacity: Career Development first). Not for anyone paying with rent or school fees.

The arithmetic

The four stages one by one come to ₦370,000. Foundation is ₦250,000 with teaching, marking and the certificate. Self-Learn buyers within 90 days pay ₦215,000 (a credit, never a discount).

The private AUDIT

Done by the participant's facilitator: their target role, recent applications and interviews, and their CV, ending with the first part to fix. Only for Foundation and above.

The four questions
  1. What do you do now, and for how long?
  2. How are you funding yourself right now?
  3. What is your date?
  4. If I show you exactly what to fix and what it costs, would you start this week?

Three clear answers: the offer. Two: one free fix, next Saturday, and a review date. One or none: the free layer, and no more sales time.
