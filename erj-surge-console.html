Diagnosis before prescription

The one sales workflow

A new lead never receives a product list. Target, 30-day signal, the evidence, the earliest weak point, one useful action, then the smallest honest door.

01 · Target role

One title or field. No role, no pricing.

02 · 30-day signal

Applications and interviews. This separates silence from mismatch from conversion.

03 · Can they do the work?

If unsure, send the free Capacity Audit first. A capacity gap is fixed by building the skill, not by a better CV.

04 · Earliest weak point

Capacity → the skill is not there yet. Supply → no legitimate roles. Representation → applications, no interviews. Aim → mismatches. Conversion → interviews, no offer.

05 · First useful action

One fix they can act on now. It proves the diagnosis before the sale.

06 · Smallest correct door

Only now: one door, its price, its limit, and a clean decision.

If 30 applications produced 0 interviews, "apply more" isn't a strategy yet.

A remote job search breaks at one of five parts:

01 Capacity — the skill for the role isn't there yet.
02 Supply — you aren't seeing enough roles you can actually take.
03 Representation — the employer can't read your value from the CV or LinkedIn.
04 Aim — you're applying where your location, level or evidence doesn't fit.
05 Conversion — interviews happen, offers don't.

Fixing all five at once is the mistake. Find the earliest weak point first.
