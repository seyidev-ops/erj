What happens in the clinic

Bring a real search. Leave knowing what to fix first.

  • 01
    Find your leak using the Four Points
    We separate Supply, Representation, Aim and Conversion so you stop trying to repair everything at once. The earliest failing point becomes the first correction.
  • 02
    Live CV + ATS teardown
    We review selected participant CVs against the ERJ ten-point rubric and show what software and a hiring manager can — and cannot — read quickly.
  • 03
    Job-advert fit check
    Bring one role you genuinely want. We check whether the company can hire across borders, whether the role fits your evidence and what the advert is actually asking you to prove.
  • 04
    Position the application before you send it
    We show how to reflect the role title, evidence, keywords and proof without turning your CV into keyword stuffing or sending forty hopeful applications.
  • 05
    Your next honest door
    You may need a free fix, the CV Engine, Self-Learn, Foundation Training, DFY or no paid ERJ service at all. The diagnosis decides — not the price list.
  • 06
    Continue in the daily Audit Rounds
    The Clinic does not end when Zoom closes. Inside the Clinic WhatsApp group, daily Audit Rounds let members bring one real application at a time, show the evidence, identify the earliest leak and act on the first fix before the next live Clinic.

Who this is for

If any of these is you, this hour was built for you.

01

You've applied and heard nothing

Dozens — maybe hundreds — of applications into silence, and you've started to wonder whether the problem is you. It isn't. You'll see exactly where the applications were dying.

02

You're earning in Naira and watching it shrink

You're good at what you do; the currency is the problem, not the competence. This is the route to a payroll that doesn't melt every quarter.

03

You don't want to japa

You want the global salary without leaving your family, your city or your country. That's precisely what remote work makes possible — and precisely what this session maps.

04

You have no connections abroad

No uncle in Canada, no friend who "referred" you. Good — the system taught here is built for people starting with no network at all.
