How it works

One walkthrough. Everyone gets a turn.

  1. 01

    The map, in a few minutes

    The five parts of a remote job search: Capacity, Supply, Representation, Aim and Conversion. So you know what we are looking for when we open your CV.

  2. 02

    Your turn, one person at a time

    We go through your CV together, live. What the software and a hiring manager can read in seconds, and what they cannot. Turns are short, so we get through everyone, and you learn from the CVs ahead of yours.

  3. 03

    Where you stand, said plainly

    Which of the five parts is weakest for you right now, and the one thing to fix first. Not ten things. One.

  4. 04

    Your next step

    Usually a free fix you can finish this week. If one paid ERJ door is truly the right one, we name it and its limit. The walkthrough decides, not the price list.

It is weekly, and that is all it is. No group to join, no course to buy to take part. Come back next Saturday with the fix done and show us.
