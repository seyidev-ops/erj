An honest comparison, written by the seller

Self-Learn or the live cohort? Same stages. Different delivery.

We sell both, so treat this with the suspicion it deserves — then check every claim on the two product pages, which carry full prices and full contents. The short version: the material is identical, and what you are actually choosing between is whether anybody is watching you do the work.

The short answer

One difference decides it. The rest is detail.

If you will genuinely do work that nobody checks, take the self-paced pack and start tonight — you would be paying ₦215,000 more for supervision you do not need. If you know from experience that unwatched work does not get done, the pack will sit unopened and the live cohort is the cheaper purchase, because a finished ₦250,000 course beats an abandoned ₦35,000 one.

Everything below is the detail behind that sentence.

Which one is you

Pick honestly. The wrong one is expensive either way.

Take the Self-Learn Pack if

  • You have finished something before that nobody was checking — and you can name it.
  • You want to start tonight rather than wait for a cohort date.
  • ₦250,000 is genuinely not available, and ₦35,000 is.
  • You do not need a certificate for anything specific.
  • You would rather have the material now and buy the live training later if you stall.

The honest bit

Three things a seller would rather not print.

The cheap one is only cheap if you finish it. An abandoned ₦35,000 pack costs more than a completed ₦250,000 cohort, because the second one produced four assets and a certificate and the first produced a folder. Most people who fail with self-paced material do not fail at the material.

Neither of these gets you a job. Both make you hireable. Neither applies on your behalf, sources roles, or sits in your interviews — that is Job Application DFY, and it is a separate purchase at a separate price. Anyone selling you training as a job guarantee is selling something else.

You might need neither this month. If you have not run the free ninety-second diagnostic, do that first. If it tells you your leak is Aim — that you are applying to companies who could never legally pay you — then no amount of CV training helps, and we would rather you knew that before paying anyone, us included.
