Why searches stall

It fails at one point. Almost nobody knows which.

Most remote job searches don't fail because the person isn't good enough. They fail at one of four points — and the months get spent repairing the other three.

02 RepresentationIf your CV can't be read, you were never really in the running.
03 AimApplying everywhere isn't the same as applying where you'd get hired.
04 ConversionInterviews don't pay you. A signed offer does.
