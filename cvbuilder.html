The marking scheme

We print the ten points rather than withhold them.

Withholding the criteria is how weaker training keeps people coming back. Here is the whole scheme. Fix your CV against it yourself, for nothing, and we would still count that a good outcome.

01A file a parser can actually open — no tables, columns, text boxes or images
02A job title line that matches the role you are applying for
03Contact details a recruiter can act on, in the body and not the header
04A summary that says what you do, not what you are passionate about
05Achievements carrying a number, a scale or a result
06Verbs at the start of bullets, not responsibility-for phrasing
07Keywords the advert itself uses, in the words the advert uses
08Evidence of remote or async work, however small
09Dates and gaps stated plainly rather than hidden by formatting
10One page per decade of work, and nothing older than it earns

The free 10-Point CV Self-Scan scores you against exactly these, in under a minute, with no payment and no email. The pass is what you buy when you already know the score and want the instrument that moves it.
