The honest part

When free is enough — and when it honestly isn’t.

Free is genuinely enough if

  • Your leak is Supply. The job channel is the whole fix, and no paid product improves on it.
  • Your CV fails two or three of the ten points and you can see exactly which. Fix them and re-scan.
  • You are aiming badly because nobody told you which companies can pay you. That is a reading problem, and the reading is free.
  • You are early, not yet applying, and building a baseline. There is nothing to diagnose yet.
  • You will do the work alone. Everything the paid method teaches, the free layer will get you a surprising distance toward.

Free stops being enough when

  • You have worked the scan and the CV still cannot carry what you have done. That is a build, not a checklist — the Self-Learn Pack or Foundation Training.
  • Only the document is broken and you want the instrument rather than a programme — the ₦5,000 pass.
  • You are already hireable and the missing input is your time, not your ability — the done-for-you routes.
  • You need someone to mark your work and tell you it is not good enough yet. Nothing free does that, here or anywhere.
  • You have read all of this and still cannot tell what is wrong. That is what AUDIT on WhatsApp is for, and it is also free.
