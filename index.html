Every job search has five parts. When offers stop, one of them is broken. We find it, fix it with you, and stay until you sign.

  1. 01CapacityCan you do the job?→We build the skill and the remote habits first.
  2. 02SupplyAre you finding jobs open to you?→We bring you real roles you are eligible for.
  3. 03RepresentationDo your CV and LinkedIn show it?→We rebuild them until a stranger sees it in 30 seconds.
  4. 04AimAre you applying where you can be hired?→We aim you at employers who hire and pay from here.
  5. 05ConversionDo interviews turn into offers?→We rehearse the interview and the pay talk with you.

07Before You Decide

The questions everyone asks first.

Should I take the self-paced pack or the live cohort?

They cover the same four stages, the same rubrics and the same four deliverables — it is the same curriculum. The live Foundation Training adds taught sessions, a marked verdict on your work, a certificate, and a cohort keeping pace; the ₦35,000 self-paced pack has none of those. The honest test is whether you will do work nobody is watching you do. We wrote the whole thing out side by side: Self-Learn vs the live Foundation Training →

What is the career-development access from The Mentorine School?

Everyone who enrols and pays for Remote Job Foundation Training, Stages 1–4 receives 90 days of access to one self-study career track at The Mentorine School, at no additional cost. Your assessment decides how you use it: if Capacity is your gap, we assign the learning and practical work that closes it; if it is not, we focus on your other gaps and the access stays open for you. The 90 days start when you activate. Not enrolling? Career Development — Self-Study is sold separately at ₦50,000.

I've applied to hundreds of jobs with no reply. How is this actually different?

Public job boards put you against 500–800 applicants and an ATS bot that rejects in seconds. We flip that: global-standard positioning first (CV, portfolio, video presence), then 30+ verified pipelines sourced weekly and applied at volume with you — with accountability check-ins until the offer lands. You stop spraying; you start targeting companies already willing to hire you.

I don't have "tech skills." Can this still work for me?

Yes. Remote roles span operations, customer success, virtual assistance, sales, admin, writing, project coordination and more. Stages 1–4 build the remote work skills every global employer screens for — async communication, digital tools, deliverable discipline — on top of the experience you already have.

How much time do I need each week?

Members have landed roles while juggling exams, full-time work and ministry. Plan for roughly 90 focused minutes a day. The system is built for busy professionals — everything is structured, templated and guided so no minute is wasted.

What does "we don't let you go until you're hired" really mean?

It's The Promise: your enrolment doesn't expire when the training ends. You keep the community, the weekly verified job listings and the accountability structure until you're hired — and the support to keep the job after you land it.

How do payments work? Is it safe?

Payments use Paystack, Selar or the official Business Play Ltd bank account depending on the product. Paystack supports cards, transfer and USSD. Flexible options exist too: you can take the programme one stage at a time from ₦70,000. The Inner Circle is ₦250,000 once, by application, on a private 1:1 basis. See every option on one page →
