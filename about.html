About Everything Remote Job

We help African professionals win remote work, from home.

Everything Remote Job (ERJ) is a career company in Abuja, run by Business Play Limited. We help professionals in Nigeria and across Africa get ready for remote roles, find the ones genuinely open to them, present their work so an employer abroad can read it, and turn interviews into offers. We know the road because we have travelled it.

How we work

Find the problem. Fix it.

Most remote job searches fail at one of five points. We find which one is yours before we suggest anything, starting with the first. Tap a point to see it.

  1. CapacityCan you do the work, remotely, without someone watching? You can't be hired for work you can't yet do.
  2. SupplyAre roles you are genuinely eligible for reaching you? You can't apply for a job you never saw.
  3. RepresentationDo your CV and profile make your real work easy to read? If your CV can't be read, you were never really in the running.
  4. AimAre you applying for roles that fit your evidence? Applying everywhere isn't the same as applying where you'd get hired.
  5. ConversionOnce the interviews come, can you turn them into an offer? Interviews don't pay you. A signed offer does.

What we promise, and what we don't

Plain terms, before you pay anything.

We will

  • Publish every price, in full, on the store.
  • Say who each programme is not for, and tell you when you don't need us.
  • Mark your work properly and tell you where it stands.
  • Stay with you. Our pledge: we will not let you go until you're hired. It is a promise to keep going, not a date.

We won't

  • Promise you a job, a start date or a salary. Employers decide those.
  • Pretend a role is open to Nigeria when it isn't.
  • Ask you to pay into a personal account. Payments go only to Business Play Ltd.
  • Charge you after you are hired. The fee pays for the work we do, which you can see.
