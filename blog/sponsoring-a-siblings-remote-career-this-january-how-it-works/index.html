Sponsoring a Sibling’s Remote Career This January: How It Works

Many of our participants are sponsored by a brother, sister, spouse or parent, often from abroad. If you are thinking of doing that for January, here is how it works.

Oluwaseyi Ashiru 13 December 2026 3 min read

December is when many families talk about the year ahead. And in many of those conversations, someone abroad, or someone at home who is doing well, asks the same question: how can I help my brother, sister or child earn properly, without just sending money every month?

A good number of the people in our cohorts were paid for by someone else. This post explains how that works and how to make sure it is money well spent.

How it works

  1. You tell us who. Their name, how to reach them, and the kind of work they do now.
  2. You pay once. Foundation Training is ₦250,000 per person.
  3. We take it from there. We contact them, confirm a class time they can actually attend, and onboard them.
  4. You hear how it is going. With their permission, we send you a short update at the end of each stage.

The next cohort, Cohort 14, begins on Monday 4 January 2027 at 8:00 PM WAT. Enrolment closes on Sunday 3 January at 8:00 PM WAT.

Make sure it will not be wasted

Paying for someone is generous. It works best when three things are true:

  • They want it. Talk to them first. Training someone did not choose rarely ends well. They have to give the classes their evenings for four weeks.
  • They have something to build on. Foundation makes people ready to work remotely in work they can already do, from customer service to finance to operations. It does not teach a profession from scratch. If they have no work experience at all, a career track first may be the better gift.
  • They can protect the time. One class a day at 7:00 PM, at 8:00 PM, or on weekend evenings, plus time for the work they submit.

What they will have at the end

A remote operating system they use every day, the common tools, writing that works across time zones, and a CV, LinkedIn profile and portfolio built for the roles they want. Their work is marked by a person. Their facilitator audits their job search privately with them. They receive the Remote Job Proficiency Certificate, and 30 days of one career track at The Mentorine School.

What we do not promise

We do not promise them a job, a date or a salary. Employers decide. What we do promise is the work above, done properly, and that we stay with them after the training ends.

Not sure it is the right fit for them?

Ask them to come to the free Launchpad on a Saturday at 4:00 PM WAT and bring their CV. Within an hour, they will know whether their gap is one fix or a full build, and so will you.
