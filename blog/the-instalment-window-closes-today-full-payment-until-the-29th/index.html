The Instalment Window Closes Today. Full Payment Until the 29th

Today, Sunday 22 November, is the last day of the instalment route for Cohort 13. What happens next, and what to do if you are still undecided.

Oluwaseyi Ashiru 22 November 2026 3 min read

Today is Sunday 22 November. It is the last day of the instalment route for Cohort 13.

What changes after today

  • From tomorrow, new registrations are by full payment only.
  • Registration stays open until the gate on Sunday 29 November at 8:00 PM WAT.
  • Classes begin on Monday 30 November.
  • The fee is the same as it has always been: ₦250,000 for all four stages.

If you already started paying

Your seat is held in your name. If you have not completed the fee by tonight, you keep every naira paid, and the balance is due in one payment before the gate. We will confirm your balance on WhatsApp if you ask.

If you meant to start and have not

You can still start today with any amount. There is no minimum. A payment that lands today holds your seat. The steps are on the instalment page.

If you are still undecided

Do not let a deadline make the decision. Ask yourself three plain questions.

  1. Do I have real work experience to build on? The training turns experience into proof an employer can read. It does not replace experience.
  2. Can I give it an hour a day for twenty training days? There are three class times, two on weekdays and one at the weekend. If none fits your life this month, wait for a cohort where one does.
  3. Is training actually my problem? If you have not checked, take the free diagnosis now. It takes about ninety seconds.

If the answers are yes, yes and yes, there is no reason to wait a further month. If any answer is no, there is no shame in that. Cohort 14 begins in January, and the free tools are open every day.

What we will not say

We will not tell you seats are running out when they are not. We will not tell you this is your last chance, because it is not. We will not promise you a job by a date, because nobody can.

What we will say is what the training does. Over twenty training days you build a CV that software and people can both read, a LinkedIn profile, a portfolio, and a weekly system for finding and applying to roles you are eligible for. You practise interviews. A person marks your work. And we keep working with you until you are hired.

Other routes, same week

  • Want the same four stages alone, at your own pace? The Self-Learn Pack is ₦35,000.
  • Short of time, with a strong CV? The Job Hunter starts at ₦15,000.
  • Not ready to pay for anything? The Remote Job Board and the CV Scan are free.

The full programme is laid out on the Foundation Training page. If you have a question that decides it for you, send it to us on WhatsApp today and a person will answer.
