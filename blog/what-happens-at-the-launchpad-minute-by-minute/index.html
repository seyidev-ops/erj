What Happens at the Launchpad, Minute by Minute

The Launchpad is free, live on Zoom, every Saturday at 4:00 PM WAT. Here is exactly what happens from the moment you register to the moment you leave.

Oluwaseyi Ashiru 26 November 2026 3 min read

People hesitate to join a live CV session because they do not know what will happen in it. Will I be put on the spot? Will strangers see my CV? Will someone try to sell me something for an hour? This post answers all of that, in order.

Before Saturday

  1. You register once. The registration is on Zoom, through the Launchpad page. Zoom emails you a personal join link. Keep that email: it is your ticket for every Saturday, not only this one.
  2. You send your CV. Step two on the same page is a short upload form. Send the CV you are using now, as it is. Do not tidy it first. The version employers are reading is the version we need to see.
  3. We read it on Friday night. Not in depth. Once. We note which of the five parts looks weakest, Capacity, Supply, Representation, Aim or Conversion, and the one thing that would make the biggest difference first.

On Saturday

From 3:30 PM the room opens. Your camera is optional, and you can stay on mute the whole time if you want to.

At 4:00 PM we start with a short map: the five places a remote job search breaks, so everyone knows what we are looking for.

Then the turns. One CV at a time. We say what a stranger abroad sees in the first few seconds, which part is holding it back, and the one thing to fix first. Turns are short so that as many people as possible get one.

Your CV goes on screen only if you agree. If you would rather not, tell us, and we talk you through it without showing it. Many people choose to watch first. That is fine. You learn a surprising amount from the CVs ahead of yours, because most of them have the same handful of problems.

At the end

We make one offer, once. If what you saw is bigger than one fix, we name the paid programme that fits, what it costs and what it does not do. If the free fix is enough for you, we say that too. Then the session ends. Nobody chases you afterwards.

After Saturday

You leave with one thing to do this week. Do it. Then come back the next Saturday with the new version, because your registration still works, and we look again.

Who it is for

  • You have applied for remote roles and heard little or nothing back.
  • You are not sure whether the problem is your CV, the roles you choose, or something else.
  • You want a person to look at your actual document, not general advice.

Who it is not for

If you have an interview booked in the next two weeks, a group session is the wrong use of your time. You need preparation for that one interview. If you need a private, one-to-one audit of your whole search, that is part of Foundation Training and the programmes above it, done by your facilitator.

Everyone else: register once, send the CV, and come on Saturday at 4:00 PM WAT.
