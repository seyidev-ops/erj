Four Days to the Gate: The Questions People Ask Before They Pay

Cohort 13 enrolment closes on Sunday at 8:00 PM WAT. These are the questions we hear most in the last week, with straight answers.

Oluwaseyi Ashiru 25 November 2026 3 min read

Every cohort, the last week brings the same questions. They are good questions. Most of them are really one question asked in different ways: will this be worth it for me? Here are the ones we hear most, with the answers we give in private.

"Will I get a job at the end?"

We cannot promise that, and anyone who does is promising something they do not control. Employers decide who they hire and when. What we can promise is that you will finish with the work a global employer looks for: a remote operating system you actually use, proof that you can work with the common tools, written communication that does not need a meeting to be understood, and a CV, LinkedIn profile and portfolio built for the market you are aiming at. Your work is marked by a person at every stage, so you know where it stands before an employer sees it.

"I work full-time. Can I keep up?"

This is the question we built the class times around. There are three: weekdays at 7:00 PM, weekdays at 8:00 PM, and weekends from 7:00 to 8:30 PM WAT. The same programme, fee and certificate on each. Pick the one you can protect. If you cannot protect any of them for the next four weeks, wait for a later cohort rather than paying for classes you will miss.

"I already know most of this."

Possibly. The test is simple. Do you have, today, a finished CV for one named role, a LinkedIn profile that matches it, one piece of work you can show a stranger, and a reply rate you are happy with? If yes, you may not need us. If any of those is missing, knowing the theory has not been enough, and that gap is what the twenty days close.

"What exactly do I get for ₦250,000?"

  • Live classes for twenty training days, Stages 1 to 4.
  • Sixteen pieces of work you submit and get marked.
  • A private audit of your job search, done by your facilitator.
  • The Remote Job Proficiency Certificate.
  • 30 days of one career track at The Mentorine School.
  • Access to the learning portal and priority support.

The four stages bought one by one would come to ₦370,000. The full programme is ₦250,000 for the same curriculum plus the teaching, the marking and the certificate.

"Can I pay in parts?"

The instalment window for this cohort closed on Sunday 22 November. If you started paying before then, you keep every naira you paid, and the balance is due in one payment before the gate. If you have not started, the remaining option for Cohort 13 is full payment before Sunday at 8:00 PM WAT.

"What if I am not sure Foundation is the right thing?"

Then do not pay this week. Come to the free Launchpad on Saturday at 4:00 PM WAT instead. Bring your CV, watch how we read it, and you will know by the end whether your problem is one fix or a full build. If it is one fix, we will say so. Cohort 14 begins on Monday 4 January 2027 if you decide later.

Who should not join this week

  • Anyone for whom the fee competes with rent or school fees.
  • Anyone who cannot give the classes their time for the next four weeks.
  • Anyone who wants to change profession completely. Foundation makes you ready to work remotely in the field you can already do. It does not teach a new field from scratch.

If none of those is you, and the questions above sound like yours, the gate closes on Sunday 29 November at 8:00 PM WAT. Cohort 13 begins the next evening.
