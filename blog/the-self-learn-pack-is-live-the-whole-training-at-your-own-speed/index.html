The Self-Learn Pack Is Live: The Whole Training, At Your Own Speed

Four stages, twenty sessions, four finished assets — the same rubrics we run with paying cohorts, now in a pack you can start today for a fraction of the fee.

Oluwaseyi Ashiru 11 August 2026 5 min read

The most common message we get is some version of the same sentence: I want the training, I cannot do the cohort right now.

Sometimes it is money. More often it is the calendar — a job that runs past eight, a shift pattern, a newborn, a term of exams. The cohort has a start date and yours has not arrived.

So we have released the self-paced edition: Remote Job Mastery Training (Self-Learn). Four stages, twenty sessions, the same four deliverables, at your own speed, from today.

What you actually get

Six items in one folder. The Self-Learn Manual, which is the course itself: twenty guided sessions with the teaching, the exercises and the specification for each deliverable. The stage slides for all four stages. The Remote Job Seeker Kit of templates and worksheets. The Smart Remote Job Prompt Pack, which is the AI prompt library plus the five-part structure underneath it so you can write your own once the pack runs out. The real participant workbook we issued to a live cohort, so you can see how the material was actually run day by day. And an invite to the private Telegram community.

At the end you are holding four things: your Remote Operating System, the Integrated Sprint, five communication assets under one index, and the Global Ready Package.

What it does not include, said plainly

No live teaching. No written verdict from a facilitator on your deliverables. No certificate. No placement support. Those are what the live cohort and Job Application DFY are, and it would be dishonest to blur the line to make this look bigger.

That means one group should not buy it: people who will not do work nobody is watching them do. If you know that about yourself, the cohort exists precisely for that reason and you should wait for it. This edition rewards a specific kind of person, and self-knowledge here saves you money.

Why the same rubrics

Because a weakened version would be a worse product and a worse advert for the real one. The ten-point CV rubric in Stage 4 is the same one our free scanner scores against and the same one our paid engine uses. There is no held-back version.

It is ₦35,000, one payment, instant download — a fraction of the cohort fee. Pay by card, transfer or USSD on Selar, or message the Registrar for the Business Play account details.

If you are not sure this is your problem at all, do not buy anything yet. Run the free ninety-second diagnostic first. If it tells you your leak is Supply, the free channel may be all you need this month.
