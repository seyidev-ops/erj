The $0 Month: What Nobody Tells You About Building a Remote Career

In one month I had two clients go silent and $0 in the pipeline with rent due in 19 days. Here's what actually turned it around — and the real timeline nobody sells you.

Oluwaseyi Ashiru 22 April 2026 6 min read

The Month That Changed Everything

In October 2022, I made exactly $0 from remote work.

I had two clients. One went silent on a Monday. The second sent a "we're pausing the project" email on a Thursday.

By Friday I had $0 in the pipeline and rent due in 19 days.

The panic didn't come immediately. First came denial. "They'll reply. Another client will appear." They didn't. One didn't.

What I Was Actually Doing Wrong

  • I had exactly two income sources — no buffer, no pipeline
  • I had never asked a single happy client for a referral
  • I had no outreach system. When work came in, I stopped looking. When it dried up, I was starting from zero
  • I confused being busy with being secure

The One Thing That Turned It Around

It wasn't a magic strategy. It was one email.

I reached out to five past clients asking one question: "Do you know anyone who might need what I do?"

Three replied. One became a referral that paid $4,200.

The thing I learned: your next client is almost always hiding in the people you've already helped. But only if you ask.

The Real Timeline

Three months from $0 back to stable. Not three weeks. Anyone selling you three weeks is selling you something.

The $0 month broke me. And it rebuilt me with a system instead of luck. Here's what the system looks like now:

  • Never fewer than 3 active or warm clients at any time
  • Monthly "thinking of you" message to 5 past clients
  • Always be in outreach mode — even when fully booked
  • 30% of income in a dedicated stability fund before touching the rest

If you're in a quiet patch right now — the answer is almost always hiding in people you've already helped. The relationship is the pipeline.
