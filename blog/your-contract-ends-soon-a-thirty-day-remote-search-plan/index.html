Your Contract Ends Soon: A Thirty-Day Remote Search Plan

When income has an end date, the search needs one too. A week-by-week plan for the thirty days before the money stops.

Oluwaseyi Ashiru 18 November 2026 3 min read

A contract with an end date gives you something most job seekers lack: a deadline. Used well, thirty days is enough to put a serious number of well-aimed applications in front of employers. Used badly, it is thirty days of scrolling and worry.

Here is the plan we give people in that position.

Before day one: decide two things

  • One target role. Not three. The role closest to what you do now is the fastest to win.
  • Your floor. The lowest monthly figure you can accept. Write it down so that you do not negotiate with yourself at midnight.

Week one: get the documents right

Do not apply yet. Every application you send this month will carry the same CV, so fix it once.

  1. Run the free CV Scan and fix every point it flags.
  2. Rewrite your two most recent roles as evidence: how much, how often, what changed.
  3. Update your LinkedIn headline to say what you do and for whom.
  4. Prepare three work examples you can tell in two minutes each.

Ask your current manager for a written recommendation now, while you are still in the building.

Week two: build the list and start

Collect twenty roles you are eligible for. Read the location line on each one first. Then send the first eight, two a day, each with the top third of your CV adjusted to the advert.

The Remote Job Board is the quickest source, because every role there is already checked as open to someone in Nigeria.

Week three: volume, with a check

Send twelve more. At the end of the week, count your replies.

  • Some replies: keep going, and spend your spare hour on interview practice.
  • No replies from twenty: stop and look. It is usually the CV, or roles that were never open to you. Do not send twenty more of the same.

Week four: convert and widen

Follow up every application older than seven days with a short, polite note. Prepare properly for any interview you have. If nothing is moving, widen to the next closest role title and to short contracts, which are often decided faster than permanent roles.

Protect the money side

  • Work out how many months your savings cover at your real spending.
  • Cut what you can before the income stops, not after.
  • Do not pay anyone who promises you a job. Nobody honest can.

Where the plan usually breaks

It breaks in week two. Finding twenty eligible roles and tailoring each application is slow, and you are still doing your current job. Most people send five and tell themselves they will catch up at the weekend.
