The Job Hunter, Explained: What One Hunt Actually Hands You

You send your CV once. We scan, score and prepare. Here is exactly what comes back within 48 hours, and who should not buy it.

Oluwaseyi Ashiru 11 November 2026 3 min read

Most people with real experience do not have a skills problem. They have an evenings problem. After a full day of work, the search is the thing that gets whatever energy is left, and the applications show it.

The Job Hunter exists for that person. This post explains what one Hunt is, step by step, so you can decide with the facts in front of you.

What you send

One message with four things: your CV, the roles you want, where you are able to work from, and the lowest pay you will accept. That is all. You do not fill in a long form and you do not sit through a call.

What we do with it

  1. We scan. We go through the Remote Job Board, where every role is open to applicants in Nigeria and checked twice a day, plus any job links you send us yourself.
  2. We score every role from 1 to 5 against your CV. How well you match, the pay, the fit and the red flags. Anything below 4 comes back marked skip, with the reason written beside it.
  3. We prepare the ones worth sending. For each of these you get a CV tailored to that job, drafted answers to its application questions, and a short message for the recruiter.
  4. A person checks it. Every line traces back to your real CV. Nothing is invented to make you sound like someone else.

Then you press Submit. Nothing goes out in your name without you.

What one Hunt contains

  • Up to 25 roles scanned and scored against your CV.
  • A full application pack for your top 3.
  • Your scored shortlist, with the reason for every yes and every skip.
  • Delivery within 48 hours.

One Hunt is ₦15,000, one payment. If you are in an active search, Hunt Month gives you a fresh hunt every week for 30 days at ₦50,000, with a tracker for what you sent and what came back.

Why the skips matter as much as the yeses

The shortlist is the part people underrate. A role that looks perfect in the title can ask for a work permit you do not have, or eight years in a field where you have three. When we mark it skip and say why, you get an evening back. Across 25 roles, that is most of a week.

The reasons also teach you something. If the same reason keeps appearing, such as a missing tool or a missing type of evidence, you have found the thing to fix before your next round.

Who it suits

  • You have real work experience and a CV that already reads reasonably well.
  • You know the kind of role you want.
  • You are short of hours, and you would rather spend them on three strong applications than thirty rushed ones.

Who should not buy it yet

  • You are not sure you can do the work. A tailored CV cannot cover a skills gap. Start with the free Capacity Audit.
  • You do not know what role you want. We can score roles against a CV, but we cannot choose your direction for you. The free diagnosis will show you where the search is stuck.
  • You want a promise. We do not promise an interview, an offer or a date. Employers decide. What changes is the quality and the aim of what you send.

A sensible way to try it

Run the free CV Scan first. If your CV scores well, one Hunt will show you within two days what a properly aimed week looks like. If it scores badly, fix that first, because every application we prepare is built on that CV.

When you are ready, start one Hunt and send your CV on WhatsApp.
