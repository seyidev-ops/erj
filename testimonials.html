Two Final-Round Rejections · Then the Offer
Tunde
Data Analyst · Lagos · 30
2 finals lost · 0 offers$46,800/year · employer of record

His CV worked. His aim worked. He reached the final round twice in three months and lost both — which meant the expensive leak was in the last twenty minutes, not the document everyone told him to rewrite again. Two things had been ending it: an answer about working unsupervised built entirely on adjectives, and a salary number he volunteered inside thirty seconds. We replaced the first with a description of his actual system — how he plans a week, where he writes things down, what he does when he is blocked and his manager is asleep — and rehearsed the money question until the silence after his range stopped frightening him. Third final round: offer, at the top of the band he named.

"I rewrote my CV four times for a problem that was never in my CV. The thing that was broken took two weeks of practice to fix."

Date / timeline

Two final-round rejections across roughly three months. The conversion leak then received focused practice; the third final round produced the offer.

Before

The CV and targeting were already working. The weak point was the interview: vague adjectives about working unsupervised and an early, unprepared salary number.

After

A concrete explanation of his real operating system — weekly planning, written work, blockers across time zones — plus rehearsed salary-range handling.

Interview / offer evidence

Two finals lost; third final round converted. Published outcome: $46,800/year via employer of record.

What ERJ specifically changed

Stopped unnecessary CV rewriting; diagnosed Conversion; rebuilt the remote-work answer around actual behaviour; rehearsed the money question until he could hold the silence and the range.
