The build · Stages 1–4

Four stages. Twenty days. Every day ends with something real.

Stage 1 · 5 days

Remote Mindset Blueprint

  • Protect your peak hours with two 90-minute deep-work blocks a day
  • Run the Pomodoro Protocol: four 25-minute sprints, no self-interruption
  • Set 3 measurable KPIs each morning and send a structured end-of-day report each evening
  • Audit your physical and digital workspace

You walk away withYour personal remote operating system: calendar blocks, KPI sheet, Pomodoro tracker and end-of-day template.

Stage 2 · 5 days

The Digital Toolkit

  • Run screen shares, annotation and chat in Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams
  • Set up cloud recordings with transcription and protected links
  • Build a structured project board in Asana, Trello or ClickUp
  • Build AI fluency: prompt craft, AI-assisted drafting and review, and knowing when not to use it
  • Co-edit documents in Suggestion Mode with full version history

You walk away withThe Integrated Sprint: a project board with 5 task cards, a linked document and a 2-minute recorded walkthrough.

Stage 3 · 5 days

Async Communication Mastery

  • Write zero-follow-up emails that never need a clarifying reply
  • Use the Batch Message Protocol in Slack and Teams
  • Keep a warm tone in writing without losing directness
  • Build your “Working With Me” manual and a tech fallback plan

You walk away with5 professional communication assets, ready from day one of a remote role.

Stage 4 · 5 days

Start Your Remote Career

  • Build a remote-first, ATS-readable CV
  • Write a LinkedIn summary recruiters find
  • Curate a 2–3 project portfolio with short read-me briefs
  • Record a 2-minute STAR-format interview answer

You walk away withThe Global Ready Package: remote-first CV (PDF), portfolio (public link) and your STAR video.

Before you enrol

Straight answers.

Will this get me a job?

No honest programme can promise you a job or a date. What we are accountable for is what employers read: your CV, your portfolio, your LinkedIn, how you write and how you interview. The market decides when the offer comes, and we keep working with you after the last class until it does.

How much time does it take?

One live session a day in the class time you choose (60 minutes on weekdays, or 90 minutes on weekends), plus the sprint work that turns each session into a deliverable. Plan for about ninety minutes a day in total.

Which cohort should I join?

The one you can attend from the first day to the last. If December is already full, register forward for Cohort 14 (Mon 4 Jan) rather than starting early and falling behind.

Can I pay now for a later cohort?

Yes. That is registering forward: pay for Cohort 14 (begins Mon 4 Jan) today and your seat is held. Tell us which cohort when you send your receipt.

What do I need?

A computer with a working internet connection, a backup for power if you can manage one, and your current CV exactly as it is. We build from there.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Complete Stages 1–4 and you receive the Remote Job Proficiency Certificate with your name and student number.

What happens after I pay?
