The build · Stages 1–4

Four stages. Twenty days. Every day ends with something real.

Stage 1 · 5 days

Remote Mindset Blueprint

  • Protect your peak hours with two 90-minute deep-work blocks a day
  • Run the Pomodoro Protocol: four 25-minute sprints, no self-interruption
  • Set 3 measurable KPIs each morning and send a structured end-of-day report each evening
  • Audit your physical and digital workspace
Stage 2 · 5 days

The Digital Toolkit

  • Run screen shares, annotation and chat in Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams
  • Set up cloud recordings with transcription and protected links
  • Build a structured project board in Asana, Trello or ClickUp
  • Build AI fluency: prompt craft, AI-assisted drafting and review, and knowing when not to use it
  • Co-edit documents in Suggestion Mode with full version history
Stage 3 · 5 days

Async Communication Mastery

  • Write zero-follow-up emails that never need a clarifying reply
  • Use the Batch Message Protocol in Slack and Teams
  • Keep a warm tone in writing without losing directness
  • Build your “Working With Me” manual and a tech fallback plan
Stage 4 · 5 days

Start Your Remote Career

  • Build a remote-first, ATS-readable CV
  • Write a LinkedIn summary recruiters find
  • Curate a 2–3 project portfolio with short read-me briefs
  • Record a 2-minute STAR-format interview answer

It works on your whole search, all five fingers: Capacity · Supply · Representation · Aim · Conversion.

Will this get me a job?

No honest programme can promise you a job or a date. What we are accountable for is what employers read: your CV, your portfolio, your LinkedIn, how you write and how you interview. The market decides when the offer comes, and we keep working with you after the last class until it does.

How much time does it take?

One live session a day in the class time you choose (60 minutes on weekdays, or 90 minutes on weekends), plus the sprint work that turns each session into a deliverable. Plan for about ninety minutes a day in total.

I do not have “tech skills”. Can this still work for me?

Yes. Remote roles span operations, customer success, virtual assistance, sales, admin, writing, project coordination and more. Stages 1–4 build the remote work skills every global employer screens for (async communication, digital tools, deliverable discipline) on top of the experience you already have.

Which cohort should I join, and can I pay now for a later one?

Join the one you can attend from the first day to the last. If this month does not work, register forward: pay now for Cohort 14 (begins Mon 4 Jan) and your seat is held. Tell us which cohort when you send your receipt.

What do I need?

A computer with a working internet connection, a backup for power if you can manage one, and your current CV exactly as it is. We build from there.

How do I pay?

Through Paystack, which takes cards, bank transfer and USSD. ERJ never receives your card details. The Enrol button on this page opens the Paystack page for Foundation Training; after you pay, send your receipt on WhatsApp and your LMS login arrives within 24 hours.

What happens after I pay?

Send your receipt on WhatsApp to +234 803 292 5957. Your LMS login arrives within 24 hours, and your Mentorine activation follows your assessment.

What is the private audit?

Your facilitator goes through your search with you, one to one: the role you are aiming for, the applications and interviews you have had, and your CV. You come out knowing which of the five parts is holding you back first, Capacity, Supply, Representation, Aim or Conversion, and what to fix. We only do this for people in the programme. If you would like to see how we read a CV before you enrol, come to the free Launchpad any Saturday at 4:00 PM WAT, where we audit CVs live.

Should I take the self-paced pack instead?

If you have finished a course on your own before, the Self-Learn Pack may serve you well. If you usually stop halfway, the live cohort is the fix. The honest comparison →

What comes after the twenty days?

Graduates can join the Inner Circle: a year of one-to-one work on your search, open only to Foundation Training alumni. It starts where your cohort ends: co-applying, interview dry-runs and offer review, for twelve months.
