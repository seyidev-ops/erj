How the board works

Three rules decide what you see.

01

Open to Nigeria, or it’s off

A role goes up only when the employer opens it to Nigeria, to Africa, or worldwide with no country exclusion. “Remote” on its own is not enough.

02

Checked every morning

Each morning the board adds new roles and re-opens every apply link. A role comes off once its deadline passes, its page closes, or it gets too old to trust.

03
