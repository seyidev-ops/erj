Plainly

What it is, and what it is not.

We prepare. You apply.

We never submit an application for you. Want us to run the whole search and send them too? That is the Placement Engine.

No invented experience.

Every line in a tailored CV comes from your own CV. A person checks each pack for anything the AI made up.

Remote is not the same as open to you.

We only score roles open to applicants in Nigeria, or the ones you send us yourself.

No promised interviews.

We improve where you apply and what you send. Employers still decide. We do not promise an interview, a job, a date or a salary.

Not sure your applications are the problem? Take the free diagnosis first: if the problem is earlier, a better application will not fix it.

Questions

Before you start.

What do you need from me?

Your current CV, the roles you are going after, the countries or time zones you can work in, and the lowest monthly pay you would accept. Five minutes on WhatsApp.

Can I send you job links I found myself?

Yes. Paste them on WhatsApp and we score and prepare those too, inside your plan's limits.

What if nothing scores 4 or above?

Then we tell you plainly, show you what is holding the scores down, and point you to the fix, such as the Capacity Audit or the CV Scan. We do not dress up a poor match.

How is this different from CV Fix?

CV Fix rebuilds your one main CV by hand, for one field. The Job Hunter takes the CV you have and tailors it to each job worth applying to, along with the answers and the message.

What happens to my CV?

We use it only for your hunt and delete your files 30 days after your plan ends.
